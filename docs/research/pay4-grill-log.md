# Pay4 Grill Log

## What Has Been Discussed So Far

- Pay4 is an online PG checkout payment mode, not POS.
- Pay4 is standalone and not nested inside Cards or EMI.
- Credit card is the funding instrument.
- The flow is card-led, not bank-selection-led.
- Supported issuers are informational before card entry.
- Demo merchant is Stanley Living.
- Demo amount is ₹40,000.
- Current demo split is ₹10,000 × 4 payments.
- Current Pay Today is ₹10,099 including ₹99 bank processing fee.
- Variant B is the CEO first-time affordability demo.
- Variant D is the repeat saved-card demo.
- The prototype is static, mocked, and not production code.
- Brochure/collateral has started as a separate artifact.
