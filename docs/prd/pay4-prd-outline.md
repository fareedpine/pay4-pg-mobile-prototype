# Pay4 PRD Outline

## Problem

Define the customer affordability and merchant conversion problem.

## Goals

Define measurable customer, merchant, issuer, and Pine Labs outcomes.

## Non-goals

Confirm exclusions such as POS, SKU-level financing, tenure selector, bank selection, and non-card funding.

## Users

Define shopper, merchant, issuer/bank partner, ops/support, settlement/finance, and internal product/engineering users.

## Customer journey

Describe first-time Pay4 selection, issuer information, card entry, consent, authentication, success, and fallback.

## Merchant journey

Describe enablement, checkout placement, settlement, reporting, refunds, and support.

## Eligibility

Define merchant, amount, issuer, BIN, card type, card product, category, risk, and geo/currency eligibility.

## Payment flow

Define Pay4 selection, pricing display, card authentication, bank booking, merchant confirmation, and idempotency.

## Repeat user flow

Define saved-card detection, preselection, Use Another Card, consent, and repeat-user eligibility.

## Failure states

Define auth failure, unsupported card, debit card, issuer downtime, booking pending, timeout, retry, and fallback.

## Refunds/cancellations

Define full refund, partial refund, cancellation timing, bank reversal, customer communication, and settlement impact.

## Settlement/reporting

Define merchant settlement, acquirer settlement, bank program fee receivables, reconciliation, reports, and dispute handling.

## Analytics

Define funnel, eligibility, Pay4 selection, card entry, auth, failure, retry, fallback, conversion, AOV, refund, and support metrics.

## Compliance/legal

Define regulated copy, consent, disclosures, data handling, issuer terms, complaints, and audit needs.

## Dependencies

Define PG, card vault/tokenization, issuer integrations, acquirer, settlement ledger, reporting, risk, ops, and sales dependencies.

## Open questions

Maintain unresolved PRD questions here, linked back to `CONTEXT.md`.

## Launch criteria

Define product, engineering, issuer, legal, compliance, ops, finance, support, GTM, and pilot readiness gates.
