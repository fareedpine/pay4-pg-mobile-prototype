# Pay4 GTM Outline

## Positioning

Pay4 is credit-card-funded affordability built into Pine Labs Online PG checkout.

## Target merchant categories

Furniture, mattresses, fashion/lifestyle, travel, healthcare/wellness, education/upskilling, eyewear, premium retail.

## Customer value proposition

Pay one-fourth today and the rest over the next 3 months using an eligible credit card.

## Merchant value proposition

Improve conversion and AOV with an affordability lever that does not make discounting the only tool.

## Sales narrative

Frame Pay4 as a standalone checkout payment mode that helps convert high-intent customers facing upfront payment hesitation.

## Objections

Capture likely merchant objections around fees, issuer coverage, settlement, refunds, customer confusion, and support load.

## FAQs

Develop customer, merchant, and sales FAQs after legal/compliance wording is approved.

## Launch plan

Define pilot merchants, issuer coverage, category focus, sales enablement, support readiness, and measurement.

## Success metrics

Track eligibility rate, Pay4 selection, auth success, conversion, AOV, refund rate, support tickets, settlement accuracy, and merchant adoption.

## Collateral needs

Brochure, one-pager, demo script, merchant FAQ, customer FAQ, sales objection guide, implementation guide.

## Open questions

Maintain GTM questions that need product/legal/commercial decisions.
